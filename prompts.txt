=== Prompt 1 of 8: Audit Your OpenClaw Setup ===

**For my agent:** Read **Before You Trust OpenClaw: Backups, Access, and a Recovery Plan** at `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xbillwatsonx/openclaw-runbook-1-before-you-trust-openclaw/v0.1.0/runbook/oc-runbook-1-before-you-trust-openclaw.md`, especially **Section 2, Read-only setup assessment**, before acting. Compare that section with my installation. This prompt authorizes read-only inspection only.

Help me understand my OpenClaw setup before I rely on it. Run the Section 2 assessment commands and report my installed version, gateway status, default or custom state/config/workspace paths, service model, and the backup commands available on this build. Describe sensitive material without printing values.

Tell me what you checked, what you found, what still needs attention, and the next safe step. Do not change files, configuration, services, access controls, or scheduled jobs.

=== Prompt 2 of 8: Map What Your Setup Depends On ===

**For my agent:** Read **Before You Trust OpenClaw: Backups, Access, and a Recovery Plan** at `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xbillwatsonx/openclaw-runbook-1-before-you-trust-openclaw/v0.1.0/runbook/oc-runbook-1-before-you-trust-openclaw.md`, especially **Section 2, Read-only setup assessment**, before acting. This prompt authorizes read-only inspection only.

Map the state directory, active configuration path, workspace, credentials location if identifiable, and gateway service for this installation. Note any custom `OPENCLAW_CONFIG_PATH` or non-default location that changes the usual assumptions. Give me a short explanation of why each item matters to recovery, without printing secret values or private file contents.

Finish with what you checked, what you found, and the next safe step.

=== Prompt 3 of 8: Confirm Independent Host Access ===

**For my agent:** Read **Before You Trust OpenClaw: Backups, Access, and a Recovery Plan** at `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xbillwatsonx/openclaw-runbook-1-before-you-trust-openclaw/v0.1.0/runbook/oc-runbook-1-before-you-trust-openclaw.md`, especially **Section 3, Confirm independent host access**, before acting. Do not create accounts, change passwords, open ports, or configure remote access.

Help me prove that I can reach the host without going through OpenClaw. Tell me how to open a direct terminal on the host, which account owns the installation, and the one safe command I should run there to confirm the state directory exists. Mark this step complete only after I confirm that I ran it in a direct host terminal.

If I cannot reach the host directly, explain the separate follow-up I need. Report what you checked and the next safe step.

=== Prompt 4 of 8: Create a Verified Backup ===

**For my agent:** Read **Before You Trust OpenClaw: Backups, Access, and a Recovery Plan** at `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xbillwatsonx/openclaw-runbook-1-before-you-trust-openclaw/v0.1.0/runbook/oc-runbook-1-before-you-trust-openclaw.md`, especially **Sections 4 and 5, Choose a backup method and destination** and **Create and verify the supported archive**, before acting.

First, run `openclaw backup --help` and tell me whether this build provides both `create` and `verify`. Then help me choose a private output directory, a private off-host destination, and whether the workspace should be included. Explain the choice and wait for my explicit approval before creating an archive.

After I approve, use the supported archive command from the runbook, verify the resulting archive again, and report its filename, size, verification result, and privacy-safe destination description. Do not print credentials or create a manual backup unless I separately approve that new plan.

=== Prompt 5 of 8: Move the Backup Off-Host ===

**For my agent:** Read **Before You Trust OpenClaw: Backups, Access, and a Recovery Plan** at `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xbillwatsonx/openclaw-runbook-1-before-you-trust-openclaw/v0.1.0/runbook/oc-runbook-1-before-you-trust-openclaw.md`, especially **Sections 4 and 5, Choose a backup method and destination** and **Create and verify the supported archive**, before acting.

Help me choose a private off-host destination for my verified archive. Explain practical choices such as a private cloud folder, external drive, NAS, or another computer I control. Ask which one I want, explain the transfer and confirmation steps, and wait for my approval before uploading or copying anything.

Once I approve, help me confirm the archive reached that private destination. Report the method, confirmation, and next safe step without printing a public link or secret values.

=== Prompt 6 of 8: Build Your Recovery Information Sheet ===

**For my agent:** Read **Before You Trust OpenClaw: Backups, Access, and a Recovery Plan** at `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xbillwatsonx/openclaw-runbook-1-before-you-trust-openclaw/v0.1.0/runbook/oc-runbook-1-before-you-trust-openclaw.md`, especially **Section 6, Make the recovery information sheet**, before acting.

Use the runbook’s recovery-sheet format to help me create a short private reference with the host, owner account, important setup locations, direct-host access route, backup method and destination, verification command, second route for help, and next maintenance date.

Ask for only the information you need. Keep secret values out of chat and the sheet. Give me plain text I can save in a private place outside `~/.openclaw`.

=== Prompt 7 of 8: Verify a Backup Safely ===

**For my agent:** Read **Before You Trust OpenClaw: Backups, Access, and a Recovery Plan** at `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xbillwatsonx/openclaw-runbook-1-before-you-trust-openclaw/v0.1.0/runbook/oc-runbook-1-before-you-trust-openclaw.md`, especially **Section 7, Verify the archive without touching the live install**, before acting.

Help me confirm that my archive exists, passes `openclaw backup verify <archive-path>`, and has a sensible contents listing. If I ask for a stronger extraction check, explain what will be written, wait for my approval, create a scratch directory, a temporary empty directory used for a safe extraction test, with `mktemp -d`, and extract only there. Clean up only that exact scratch directory after I confirm the result.

Report what you checked, what you found, and the next safe step. Do not restore over the live installation.

=== Prompt 8 of 8: Record Your Maintenance Plan ===

**For my agent:** Read **Before You Trust OpenClaw: Backups, Access, and a Recovery Plan** at `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xbillwatsonx/openclaw-runbook-1-before-you-trust-openclaw/v0.1.0/runbook/oc-runbook-1-before-you-trust-openclaw.md`, especially **Sections 8 and 9, Explain limits and set maintenance** and **Completion criteria and stop conditions**, before acting.

Help me write a short maintenance plan. It should say what I have in place, any practical limits I should remember, my next maintenance date about a month from now, and what I’ll do then: create a fresh backup, verify it, confirm the off-host copy, re-check direct host access, and update my recovery sheet if the setup changed.

Offer to set a reminder if my agent supports it, but wait for my approval before creating one. Keep the result short, clear, and free of secret values.
